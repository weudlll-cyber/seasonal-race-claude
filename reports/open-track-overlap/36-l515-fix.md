## ARCHITECTURE.md invariant update

Invariant 6 now reads:

> **There are NO raw physicalY↔px conversions anywhere.** The previous "L515 exemption" (`frameSizePx / trackWidth` in the free-lane sensor) was fixed here: `/ trackWidth` gave HALF a frame in physicalY (off by 2×), creating a blind zone where bodies overlapped visually but the sensor missed them (report 35). All lateral thresholds now go through `pxToPhysicalY` / `physicalYToPx`. If you see `/ trackWidth` or `* trackWidth` next to a physicalY quantity, it is a bug.
