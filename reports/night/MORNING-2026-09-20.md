## ★ THREE THINGS THAT ARE NOT WHAT THE BRIEF ASSUMED

**1 · `sim-fairness.mjs` cannot run this chain.** Timed, not estimated: **353 s for 2 races on one
track** (~176 s/race) — tens of hours for eight arms. The gate is computed instead from the project's
own functions, `bandOfRank` and `computeFairnessStats`, inside the fast physics harness, which the
brief's §D authorises.

**2 · The two gate routes do not demonstrably agree at the N the validation could afford.**
One track (dirt-oval), shipped control:

| route | band arrival |
|---|---|
| `sim-fairness.mjs` (the project's gate), 3 races | **93.3%** |
| `bandOfRank` in the physics harness, 3 races | **85.0%** |

★ The two runs are **not the same three races** — `sim-fairness` with `--seed=0` draws random seeds —
so this is two small samples of one world, and 8 points on 120 racer-slots is a handful of racers.
**Per §K the conservative reading is taken: every gate figure in these reports is labelled MY
measure, not the project's gate.** The chain carries on, as the rule directs.

**3 · The whole-race breakaway maximum cannot separate these arms**, and was caught before the run:
four of six arms returned an identical 143.964 px because the peak falls at progress **0.1518** —
`pulkStartFrac` is 0.15, so it is the CHAOS spread, in a phase no arm touches. The headline is the
fixed **[0.70, finish]** window, as §B requires.
