## THE FOUR THINGS THAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST SHEET

**1 · ★★ THE REPLAY WORKS.** The single most valuable open question in the chain was whether a
disputed race can actually be re-run from its record. **It can.** `W57FQA` from your own database —
seed 9, 40 racers, `quiet` — replayed **40 of 40 positions and 40 of 40 finishing times identical
to the millisecond**. It settles *"did the engine do this"*; it can never settle *"did this
happen"*, because replaying a fabricated result would reproduce the fabrication faithfully.

`scripts/diag/line-ceiling-terms.mjs` has not parsed since `becc455c` on 2026-08-22 — a retirement
commit removed a `console.log`'s arguments and left the opening call behind. Found by the second
adversarial run, not by piece 7. Repaired; it now parses and runs. **All 278 scripts parse.**

**3 · ★★ TWO DEPLOYMENT DOCUMENTS HANDED YOU A COMMAND THAT DOES NOT EXIST.** `DEPLOYMENT.md` and
`ENVIRONMENT.md` both said to run `node scripts/check-bundle-address.mjs`. The script is
`audit-bundle-address.mjs`, and its own header explains the `audit-` prefix is deliberate:
renaming it to match the docs would auto-register it as a routed guard and redden `verify` on every
tree without a fresh client build. Repaired in both documents and in one source comment.

**4 · ★ I MISTOOK A CRASH FOR A CLEAN RESULT.** Piece 5 reported "server: 0 unused variables" from
`npx eslint` run in `server/`. That command **crashes** — `server/` has no eslint of its own — and I
read its empty warning list as a zero. The conclusion survived re-testing with the right
instrument, and a sabotage proved it can fail, but the method sentence was wrong and is corrected
in place. **It is the exact failure this report's own evidence standard names, committed inside the
audit that names it.**
