### Two defects found, recorded as a new PART ONE row, not changed

1. **`POST /api/races/:shortKey/verify` can stop the server.** Any error other than a refusal is
   rethrown from an `async` handler (`server/src/routes/races.js:275`); Express 4 does not catch a
   rejected promise, and the server has no error middleware and no `unhandledRejection` handler, so
   under Node's default the process exits. Admin-only.
2. **The stored-race duplicate check is not team-scoped** (`server/src/races/raceStore.js:500`):
   another team's `clientRaceId` is answered with that race's `id` and `shortKey`. The ids are
   random, so it is hard to reach.

Also noticed while documenting, and left (all in API.md where the route is): only `/api/races` is
scoped by team — tracks, brands, racers, player groups and surface classes are shared and operators
may change them; three deletes (`DELETE /api/brands/:id`, `/api/brands/:id/logo`,
`/api/racers/:id`) skip the filename check the track deletes have; `PUT /api/brands/:id` stores a
`null` subtitle or sponsor text as the string `"null"`; with no error handler, a malformed JSON body
or a failed disk write answers with Express's HTML page, not JSON.
