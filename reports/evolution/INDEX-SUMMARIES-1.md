> *"all seven intrusions (31–192 px²) sit inside that budget"*

**Seven is the same inflated pass.** If the collision count falls from 12 boxes to 11 and the hits
from 7 to 3, the intrusion count cannot survive untouched.

**No report gives a corrected intrusion count, so none is invented here.** LABEL-OVERLAP-FIX-1
corrects the *"7 of 12"* and says the mechanism, the name-vs-number signature and the PHOTO_FINISH

to the block.** The px² range stands on its own: it is a measurement of individual overlaps and does
not depend on how many there were.

**A sweep that finds a second site four lines from the first is a sweep that was needed.** The
brief's constraint says uncapped, and this is what uncapped bought.

LABELS-AND-FLOOR-1 and MINIMAP-ONE-SOURCE-1. **All five are append-only by project rule.** The

**The dated entries INSIDE the corrections block are also left as written**, and this is the one
place a reader might expect a fix. The block is chronological: the 2026-08-22 entry that corrects
*"7 of 12" → "3 of 11"* sits **above** the 2026-08-22 entry that states *"7 of 12"* while withdrawing
something else. A reader who reaches the second has already passed the first. **Correcting a dated
entry in place is how a correction record stops being a record.**

**The INDEX's own summaries are not reports**, which is the distinction this piece rests on. Nothing
had ever corrected them because nobody had noticed they were a separate surface.
