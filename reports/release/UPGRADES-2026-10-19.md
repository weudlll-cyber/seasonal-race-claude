### Piece 4 — Express 5 · MERGED `d80dc945`

| package | from | to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| express | 4.22.2 | 5.3.0 (router 2.2.0, path-to-regexp 8.4.2, body-parser 2.3.0) |

The server suite passed unchanged on Express 5 (1107 of 1107) — and that was not the bar. Each
change in Express's migration guide was checked against this server, and three reached it:

1. **`req.body` is undefined when no parser read a body** ("req.body"; Express 4 gave `{}`). Six
   routes then answered **500** to a request with no body — `POST /api/tracks`, `/api/player-groups`,
   `/api/brands`, `/api/racers`, `/api/surface-classes` and `PUT /api/surface-classes/:id` — found by
   sending every body-reading route a request with no body (no test did). `app.js` now restores the
   `{}` they were written to, in one place. New `noBody.integration.test.js`: no body is answered
   exactly as `{}` (status and body), never 5xx — 17 routes; red in 8 without the fix.
2. **`app.listen` hands a listen error to its callback** ("app.listen"). `index.js`'s callback would
   have printed "RaceArena server running" on a taken port and kept the process alive serving
   nothing (Express 4 crashed with exit 1). `listenOn` (bindAddress.js) now splits the outcome into
   `onListening` / `onError`, and `index.js` refuses to start: `RaceArena cannot start: listen
   EADDRINUSE …`, exit 1 — tested, and run end to end on a port held for the purpose.
3. **`res.status` throws on a status that is not a whole number from 100 to 999** ("res.status"):
   `apiErrorHandler` now takes whole numbers only (a `404.5` would have thrown inside the error
   handler); tested, and a rejected `async` handler is answered as JSON by it.

`server/utils/asyncRoute.js` (the Express 4 workaround for rejected handlers) is **kept on purpose**:
under 5 a rejection would reach `apiErrorHandler` and be answered `HTTP 500` instead of the route's
own `internal error`, which the client shows. Its header says so.
