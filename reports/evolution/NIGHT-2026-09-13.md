## 3 · ★ THE CAMERA LOSES THE FINISH LINE — CAUSE ESTABLISHED, FIX STOPPED

**Method.** `scripts/check-runin-frame.mjs`, its own ten tracks at its own field sizes, run at four
commits. One seed per track (the guard says so itself), so this is a per-RACE behaviour.

| commit | result |
|---|---|
| `b6d77637` master | **PASS** — all ten FINDABLE, 0 outside the region |
| `983d9201` the comebacker is HELD and RELEASED | **1 failure** — luger-hill n=100 |
| `57db4669` branch tip before tonight | 1 failure — the same one |
| `1997498a` HEAD, after tonight's race change | ★ **2 failures** — luger-hill n=100 **and garden-path n=40** |

★★ **THE CAUSE IS `983d9201`**, whose parent is master and master is green. No camera file was
touched on this branch at all — the RACE changed, and `983d9201` is the only commit here that moves
the default race. Everything since (the arrival variants, the servo) was measured default-off and
fingerprint-proven identical until piece 2 shipped it.

★ **AND TONIGHT'S CHANGE ADDED THE SECOND CASE.** garden-path n=40 was FINDABLE at `57db4669` and is
LOST at `1997498a`. That is a cost of the servo/arrival change and it is recorded as one.

★ **BOTH FAILURES ARE ONE MECHANISM, not two coincidences** — the signature is identical:

| track | field | progress | state | binding term | worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| garden-path | 40 | **0.950** | **LEADER_ZOOM** | `state` | −107 px, 3 off canvas |
| luger-hill | 100 | **0.950** | **LEADER_ZOOM** | `state` | −146 px, 5 off canvas |

★ **WHY `state` IS THE BINDING TERM, AND WHY THAT IS THE DEFECT.** `_binding` starts at `'state'` and
is only replaced when some ceiling is SMALLER. `_lineCeiling` returns **`Infinity` whenever the line
cannot be framed from the anchor at all** — its own header says so. An infinite ceiling is never
smaller than anything, so it never binds and never gets named: the leader shot zooms to its own
preference and the line leaves the canvas, while the probe truthfully reports that the STATE chose
the zoom. **The one case the guard exists to catch is the one case its ceiling goes quiet for.**

★★ **STOPPED, BY THE CHAIN'S OWN RULE.** The fix is to make an unframeable line do something other
than stop constraining — in `_lineCeiling` or in the LEADER_ZOOM bounding. Either **changes the
camera on every track and every race**, not these two. The chain says: *if the fix would change the
camera elsewhere, STOP and REPORT — he judges the picture.* So it is reported, not made.

## 4 · ★★ THE FAIRNESS GUARANTEE IS A 40-RACER FACT — AND NOT WHERE IT WAS THOUGHT TO BE

**Method.** `sim-fairness.mjs --hero-map` via `scripts/exp-band-scale.mjs`: ten tracks, each at its
OWN `defaultRacerTypeId`, × {20,40,60,100} racers × **20 races — 44 000 racers**. `bandReach` is the
sim's own number, READ from `hero-map.json`. The scaled arm re-scores the SAME races with a different
table, so the two columns differ only in the table. Shipped world (`RA_SERVO_RESPONSE` unset).

**RE-VERIFIED, and one of the two claims does not hold.**

★ `docs/FAIRNESS.md` **never states a field size** — confirmed, zero matches for any field-size
expression, and its "N" means RACES throughout (`N=300 races/track`).

★★ **BUT BAND-REACH DOES NOT FALL TO 70.5% AT A HUNDRED RACERS.** Measured:

| N | band-reach | ±SE | margin over the 70% gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 91.3% | 0.45 pp | 21.3 pp |
| 40 | 89.7% | 0.34 pp | 19.7 pp |
| 60 | 86.9% | 0.31 pp | 16.9 pp |
| 100 | **88.9%** | 0.22 pp | **18.9 pp** |

**The "half a point of margin" premise is not reproduced.** There are nineteen points.

★★ **THE WORRY WAS RIGHT; IT LIVES IN A DIFFERENT NUMBER.** `BAND_EDGES = [5,15,25,40]` exhausts the
field at exactly forty. Above that **B5 becomes an unbounded catch-all** and swallows an
ever-larger share of the field — and it is trivially easy, because it is 60 ranks wide:

| N | B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 | B5 | B5's share of the field |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 92.5% | 91.3% | 90.0% | — | — | 0% |
| 40 | 87.0% | 88.4% | 86.0% | 94.0% | — | 0% |
| 60 | 83.1% | 83.9% | 81.3% | 84.6% | 93.9% | 33% |
| 100 | ★ **74.1%** | 75.6% | ★ **73.5%** | 79.6% | **97.3%** | ★ **60%** |

★ **THE BANDS THAT ACTUALLY CONSTRAIN DELIVER 76.4% AT N=100**, down from 89.7% at forty. The 88.9%
headline is an average with a free band in it.

★ **AND BANDS THAT SCALE WITH THE FIELD DO RECOVER IT** — the measured arm:

| N | shipped, B1–B4 only | scaled table, all bands |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | 89.7% | 89.7% ← the control: scaling reproduces the shipped table exactly at forty |
| 60 | 83.4% | 81.4% |
| 100 | **76.4%** | ★ **82.1%** |
| 20 | 91.3% | 72.2% |

**Scaling helps exactly where the table breaks (+5.7 pp at a hundred) and hurts where it does not
(−19 pp at twenty, where the shipped table has no B4/B5 to inflate anything).**

★ **A THIRD FINDING NOBODY ASKED FOR: TODAY'S WORLD ALREADY FAILS THE GATE'S OTHER CLAUSE.**
`docs/FAIRNESS.md` requires **zero Holm-unfair start rows, every track**. Measured on the shipped
world: **0 unfair at N=20, 2 at N=40, 3 at N=60, and SEVEN OF TEN at N=100** (`luger-hill` and
`searound` unfair at every size above twenty). The gate as written is not met by the shipped game
above forty racers.

> ★ **What it means for a hundred-racer race today, in one line:** three fifths of your field is
> drawn into a single unbounded band that is 97% easy, the four bands that mean anything deliver
> 76%, and seven of ten tracks fail the start-row fairness clause.

**REPORT ONLY. `BAND_EDGES` unchanged, `docs/FAIRNESS.md` unedited.**
