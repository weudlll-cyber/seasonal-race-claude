# Proposals — index

Concept proposals and their independent reviews. **One line per file: what it proposes, and what
became of it.**

**Why this directory is indexed and the other archives are not** (INDEX-COVERAGE-1, 2026-08-18): it
is the one place under `reports/` that still RECEIVES work. `PROJECT-AUDIT-2026-08-18.md` was written
here on 2026-08-17, sat untracked and outside every guard, and was the only copy of the finding that
first-admin setup could not succeed. It was rescued into `reports/evolution/` the next morning. A
directory that new work lands in needs the orphan check; the closed archives do not.

**Same rule as the other indexes:** these are lab-journal entries, **append-only**. A proposal
records what was believed on the day it was written. Where one was measured and killed, the verdict
lives in `reports/evolution/`, not here.
